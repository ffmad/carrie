# Carrie: video script (about 75 seconds)

For the jury. Three parts: what Carrie is, the call, what each person ends up with.

Narrator **A** is Ash, narrator **B** is Quentin. Rose is played by Ash; say so in a corner caption.

## 1 · What Carrie is (0:00–0:10)

Over the phone ringing with "Carrie is calling".

> **A:** One in five people in Taiwan is over 65. Their families care, but often don't know what to ask.
> **B:** Carrie is an AI helper who calls them, listens, and carries what they choose to share to their family and their doctor.

## 2 · The call (0:10–0:50)

The recorded call, cut to these beats, with captions burned in:

1. Carrie says she is an AI in her first sentence.
2. Rose: she baked apple cake this morning, and her knee has been bad on the stairs since Sunday.
3. Carrie asks whether she can tell Dr. Chen about the knee. Rose says yes.
4. Rose: "Can you tell Leo to come early Saturday?"
5. Hang up. Hold "Here's what I'll pass on" for two seconds.

No narration over the call. Carrie never asks about the cake: everyday news needs no question.

## 3 · What each person ends up with (0:50–1:10)

Stills are in `video/` (not in the repo); or record the screens live.

- **Mia** (`/family/mia/carrie`, 6 s). **B:** *Mia hears about the apple cake, in her mum's own voice. Not the knee: Rose only said yes for the doctor.*
- **Leo** (`/family/leo/carrie`, 6 s), tap play on "Asks Leo to come early Saturday". **B:** *Leo gets her message, and a reason to call.*
- **Dr. Chen** (`/doctor`, 6 s). **A:** *Her doctor sees the knee before the visit, in her exact words.*

## 4 · Close (1:10–1:15)

> **A:** Carrie. She doesn't replace your call. She gives you a reason to make it.

(Figure for 2025: Taiwan 20.06% aged 65 or over.)

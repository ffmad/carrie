# Carrie

**▶ Demo video: https://github.com/ffmad/carrie/releases/download/demo-video/carrie-demo.mp4**

Carrie is an AI care go-between for an older person living alone, her family and her doctor. Carrie phones Grandma for a real conversation (OpenAI GPT-Live 1, full duplex). Every fact she keeps is Rose's own words, with the recording to prove it. Everyday news flows to the family; anything about her health waits for Rose's yes, one person at a time. Carrie remembers the week through GBrain, where every fact is saved with its source.

*She doesn't replace your call. She gives you a reason to make it.*
